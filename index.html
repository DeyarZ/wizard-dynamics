How much does an app cost?

Most revenue-ready v1 apps land between €25k and €60k, depending on platforms, backend and AI features. You get a fixed quote after the Blueprint week — no hourly surprises.

How fast can you launch?

Typically 6–10 weeks from kickoff to App Store. We work in weekly sprints and ship a TestFlight build every Friday, so you can test the real app from week two.

Who owns the code and the App Store account?

You do — 100%. The app is published under your developer account, the repository is yours, and all IP transfers to you. No lock-in.

Native or cross-platform?

Both. We build native iOS in SwiftUI when performance and platform feel matter most, and Flutter when you need iOS and Android from one codebase. We'll recommend the one that fits your budget and market.

We already have a live app. Can you help?

Yes — that's our Growth Partner engagement. We start with a paywall, onboarding and acquisition audit, then run experiments on what moves trial-starts and revenue. Often the fastest wins are on the paywall, not in new features.

Why should we trust you with growth?

Because we do it with our own money every day. Our portfolio runs on Apple Search Ads and Meta campaigns we manage ourselves, so we know current costs and conversion rates — not last year's case-study numbers.

Do you work for equity or revenue share?

For selected projects we combine a reduced fee with a revenue share. Tell us about your idea on the call and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

When are we the wrong choice?

If you need the cheapest possible build, an app with no business model behind it, or a team that just executes a spec without pushing back — we're not your studio. We take on a few projects per quarter and only the ones we believe can make money.

What happens after launch?

That's where most agencies stop and where we get started. Either we continue as your growth team, or we hand over cleanly with documentation, dashboards and a playbook your team can run.
