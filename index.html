What's the catch with the free blueprint?

There isn't one. Some founders take the plan and build it themselves, and that's fine with us. Most ask us to build it, because we're the ones who wrote it.

Why only five spots a week?

We research your market before the call, and a founder runs every call. Five a week is what we can do properly next to the apps we run.

Why have you built so many apps?

Because we test ideas the cheap way: ship a small version, read the numbers, and scale only what earns. Most of our 100+ apps stayed small experiments. Grass Identifier, Everlens and Noor Stories are the ones that earned their way up. Your blueprint runs your idea through the same filter before you spend real money.

What does an app cost?

That depends on platforms, backend and AI features, so we don't quote blind. At the end of your blueprint call you get a fixed price and a fixed launch date, with no hourly billing.

How fast can you launch?

Usually 6 to 10 weeks from kickoff to the App Store. We work in weekly sprints and send a TestFlight build every Friday, so you can use the real app from week two.

Who actually builds my app?

The team behind Grass Identifier, Everlens and Noor Stories. One of the two founders leads your project from the first call to launch, and you talk to the people building it, not to an account manager.

Who owns the code and the App Store account?

You do, completely. The app is published under your developer account, the repository is yours, and all IP transfers to you. There's no lock-in.

Native or cross-platform?

Both. We build native iOS in SwiftUI when performance and platform feel matter most, and Flutter when you need iOS and Android from one codebase. We'll recommend the one that fits your budget and market.

We already have a live app. Can you help?

Yes. Start with the free App Audit: we go through your paywall, onboarding and App Store page and rank the biggest revenue leaks. If you want, we then run the fixes and the experiments with you every month.

Why should we trust you with growth?

Because we've already paid for the lessons with our own money: more than $65k on Meta ads so far, plus Apple Search Ads and organic TikTok. We know this month's costs and conversion rates, not last year's.

Do you work for equity or revenue share?

For selected projects we combine a reduced fee with a revenue share. Tell us about your idea on the call and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.
