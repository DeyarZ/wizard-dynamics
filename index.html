How much does an app cost?

Most first versions that are built to earn land between €25k and €60k, depending on platforms, backend and AI features. You get a fixed quote after the blueprint week, with no hourly billing.

Have you built apps for clients before?

Client work is new for us. Our track record is our own 18 apps, and every number on this page comes from our own App Store Connect and RevenueCat accounts. That's why our first three clients get founding terms.

How fast can you launch?

Usually 6 to 10 weeks from kickoff to the App Store. We work in weekly sprints and send a TestFlight build every Friday, so you can use the real app from week two.

Who owns the code and the App Store account?

You do, completely. The app is published under your developer account, the repository is yours, and all IP transfers to you. There's no lock-in.

Native or cross-platform?

Both. We build native iOS in SwiftUI when performance and platform feel matter most, and Flutter when you need iOS and Android from one codebase. We'll recommend the one that fits your budget and market.

We already have a live app. Can you help?

Yes, that's the growth partner engagement. We start with an audit of your paywall, onboarding and acquisition, then run experiments on what moves trial starts and revenue. The fastest wins are usually on the paywall.

Why should we trust you with growth?

Because we spend our own money on it every day. Our apps run on Apple Search Ads and Meta campaigns that we manage ourselves, so we know this month's costs and conversion rates.

Do you work for equity or revenue share?

For selected projects we combine a reduced fee with a revenue share. Tell us about your idea on the call and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

What happens after launch?

Either we continue as your growth team, or we hand over cleanly with documentation, dashboards and a playbook your team can run.
